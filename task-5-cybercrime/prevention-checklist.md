This checklist provides specific, actionable tips to help college students in India recognize and avoid online threats.
*General Digital Safety
Enable Two-Factor Authentication: Enable 2-factor authentication on your university email, WhatsApp, and social media accounts.

Use Strong, Unique Passwords: Use a combination of letters, numbers, and special characters. Do not use the same password everywhere.

Be Cautious of Phishing: Do not click on any unsolicited links sent via SMS, Email, or any messaging app. These may be phishing attempts claiming your account has been suspended.

Be Cautious of App Permissions: Only download apps from Google Play Store or Apple App Store. Also, be aware of what permissions an app requests from you.

Secure Your Social Media: Keep your social media profiles private. Do not accept connection requests from complete strangers.

Update Your Phone’s Software: Update your phone’s operating system and antivirus regularly. This will ensure you don’t fall victim to any known vulnerabilities in older software versions.

*Financial & UPI Safety

Never Use UPI PIN to Receive: Remember, you don’t need to enter your UPI PIN to receive a payment. You don’t need to scan a QR code to receive a refund either.

Take UPI PIN Seriously: Do not reveal your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank passwords to anyone over a phone call. Even if they claim to be bank officials.
