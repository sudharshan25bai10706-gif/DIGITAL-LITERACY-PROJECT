# DIGITAL-LITERACY-PROJECT
Project Overview

- This project is all about the role and responsibilities of a student digital ambassador, specifically in creating and promoting awareness about digital literacy. It includes the essential components of digital literacy such as online safety, good online behavior, communication, and issues in the cyber world.

TASKS

 Task 1: Infographic
- I will design an interesting infographic using Canva to introduce some ideas related with digital literacy and online safety.

Task 2: Digital Portfolio
- I created professional profiles for myself on platforms such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and Stack Overflow.

Task 3: Platforms
- researched coding platforms such as HackerRank to enhance coding problem-solving abilities. Created a Google Form quiz to collect data and raise awareness among users.

Task 4: Communication
- devloped knowledge on how to write professional emails. Also, learned about the importance of communication etiquette in a digital world.

 Task 5: Cyberbullying Awareness
- Learned about cyberbullying, its effects on people, and various types of cyberbullying on social media and online platforms. Created awareness among people and suggested ways to avoid cyberbullying.
